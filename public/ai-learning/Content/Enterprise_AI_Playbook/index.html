How to use this library.
  1. Read the guides in order — Foundations sets the data, Applied is the operating habits, Advanced is the strategic reframe. Each takes 15–20 minutes.
  2. Drill with flashcards while reading — the deck has a filter for the current guide, so you can lock in the atoms as you go.
  3. Take the full quiz after all three — many questions are scenario judgments ("your VP says X…", "your rollout stalls…") that test whether the principles transfer.
  4. Use the 10-findings table from Guide 01 as your reference card — print it, paste it in your team's AI strategy doc, or keep it open during the next steering review.
