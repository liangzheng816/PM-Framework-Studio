# PMFrame.works research workbook

## 

## Group 1: User insights / 用户洞察

1. **Design Thinking / 设计思维**   
Human-centered cycle of empathize → define → ideate → prototype → test. Best for starting with messy user problems and iterating toward a promising concept.
2. **User Journey Map / 用户旅程地图**   
Maps the end-to-end user experience across stages, touchpoints, emotions, and friction points. Best for seeing where experience breaks and where redesign will matter most.
3. **Empathy Map / 同理心地图**   
Structures what users say, think, feel, and do so teams stop projecting their own assumptions. Best for synthesizing interview findings into a shared user picture.
4. **JTBD / JTBD（任务驱动）**   
Treats adoption as users ‘hiring’ a product to get a job done. Best for uncovering real demand beyond features and demographics.
5. **Five-Layer User Interview / 用户访谈五问法**   
A structured deep-interview approach that moves from behavior to motivations and unmet needs. Best for exploratory research when surface answers are misleading.
6. **Kano Model / Kano 模型**   
Classifies features into must-haves, performance needs, and delighters. Best for feature prioritization when not all improvements create equal user value.
7. **Service Blueprint / 服务蓝图**   
Connects frontstage user experience with backstage processes, systems, and handoffs. Best for services or multi-team workflows where the visible journey depends on hidden operations.
8. **Contextual Inquiry / 情境调查法**   
Observes users in their real environment instead of relying only on recalled stories. Best for understanding workarounds, context, and tacit behavior.
9. **Affinity Diagram / 亲和图（KJ 法）**   
Clusters raw qualitative observations into themes and patterns. Best right after interviews, field notes, or open-ended surveys.
10. **Diary Study / 日记研究法**   
Asks users to log behavior, context, and feelings over time. Best for longitudinal habits, irregular journeys, or behavior that changes by situation.
11. **Empathy Roadmap / 同理心路线图**   
Extends empathy work across time and stages so teams can track how a user’s mindset shifts during a journey. Best for emotionally sensitive or multi-step experiences.
12. **Persona / 用户画像（Persona）**   
Turns research patterns into a concrete archetype with goals, constraints, and context. Best for aligning teams on who the product is really for.



## Group 2:Problem framing / 问题定义

1. **Musk’s Five-Step Method / 马斯克五步法**   
Operational first-principles-style simplification: challenge requirements, delete parts, simplify, accelerate, automate. Best for overbuilt processes or products with legacy complexity.
2. **5 Whys / 5 Why 分析**   
Repeatedly asks why a problem happened until the team reaches a root cause. Best for incident review or operational troubleshooting when symptoms keep recurring.
3. **HMW Problem Frame / HMW 问题框架** \[Site+Mirror | note: original site places it under problem framing; TW mirror groups it under ideation]  
Converts insights into ‘How might we…?’ opportunity statements that preserve room for solutions. Best when research is rich but design direction is still fuzzy.
4. **POV Statement / POV 陈述**   
Frames a design problem as user + need + insight. Best for narrowing from broad research into a solvable product challenge.
5. **Problem Tree / 问题树**   
Breaks a problem into causes, sub-causes, and effects. Best when stakeholders keep mixing root causes with downstream symptoms.
6. **Iceberg Model / 冰山模型**   
Looks below visible events to patterns, structures, and mental models. Best for recurring organizational or market problems that keep resurfacing.
7. **Double Diamond / 双钻模型**   
Separates divergent/convergent work in both problem space and solution space. Best for teams that habitually jump from request to solution too early.
8. **Root Cause Analysis / 根本原因分析**   
Systematic family of methods for isolating underlying causes rather than patching symptoms. Best when a problem has multiple interacting drivers.
9. **Cynefin Framework / Cynefin 框架**   
Sorts situations into clear, complicated, complex, chaotic, and disorder so teams use the right decision mode. Best when people disagree because the problem type itself is unclear.
10. **Task Framing / 任务界定法**   
Clarifies the actual task, boundary, and success condition before solutioning begins. Best when a request is vague or loaded with assumptions.
11. **Mental Models / 心智模型**   
Surfaces the assumptions users or teams already carry into a situation. Best for products where misconceptions or expectations shape behavior.
12. **CIRCLES Method / CIRCLES 方法**   
A structured product-design answer format for clarifying users, problems, priorities, and tradeoffs. Best for interview-style product reasoning or rapid concept evaluation.
13. **Working Backwards / 反向工作法**   
Starts with the customer-facing future state—often a press release/FAQ—and works backward into product scope. Best for new-product definition and strategic alignment.
14. **Event Storming / 事件风暴**   
Collaboratively maps domain events, actors, commands, and rules to reveal system complexity. Best for ambiguous business processes and domain-heavy systems.
15. **PESTLE Analysis / PESTLE 分析**   
Scans political, economic, social, technological, legal, and environmental forces. Best for external-environment risk/opportunity framing.
16. **SWOT / SWOT 分析**   
Balances internal strengths/weaknesses with external opportunities/threats. Best for strategic reviews, market entry, or portfolio decisions.
17. **Competitor Analysis / 竞品分析框架**   
Compares direct, indirect, and substitute competitors across positioning, features, business model, and gaps. Best for differentiation and market narrative work.



## Group 3:Ideation / 创意生成

1. **SCAMPER / SCAMPER**   
Generates ideas by substituting, combining, adapting, modifying, repurposing, eliminating, and reversing. Best when teams need structured creativity instead of blank-sheet brainstorming.
2. **Six Thinking Hats / 六顶思考帽**   
Makes a group evaluate an idea through shared lenses like facts, risks, creativity, and emotion. Best for meetings that otherwise devolve into mixed-mode arguing.
3. **Reverse Brainstorming / 逆向头脑风暴**   
Starts from ‘how could we make this fail?’ and flips the answers into improvements. Best for surfacing hidden risks and blind spots.
4. **Crazy 8s / Crazy 8s**   
Forces eight sketches in eight minutes to unlock range before convergence. Best for UI/UX concept exploration.
5. **Analogical Thinking / 类比思维**   
Borrows patterns from other industries or domains and adapts them back to the current problem. Best for getting beyond same-industry clichés.
6. **Value Proposition Canvas / 价值主张画布**   
Matches customer jobs, pains, and gains to products, pain relievers, and gain creators. Best for clarifying why the offer matters and to whom.
7. **How-Now-Wow Matrix / How Now Wow 矩阵**   
Sorts ideas by originality and feasibility into safe, creative, and breakthrough buckets. Best after ideation when deciding which concepts to mature.
8. **Opportunity Solution Tree / 机会解决方案树**   
Links desired outcomes to opportunities and then to solution ideas and tests. Best for keeping experimentation tied to a measurable objective.
9. **Story Spine / 故事骨架法**   
Uses narrative structure to make ideas easier to explain, align around, and remember. Best for pitches, concept communication, and onboarding stakeholders.
10. **TRIZ Innovation Matrix / TRIZ 创新矩阵**   
Uses contradiction patterns and solution principles from patent analysis to solve hard tradeoffs. Best for engineering-heavy or constraint-rich innovation.
11. **Pain-Solution Matrix / 痛苦解决方案矩阵**   
Maps painful user problems against candidate solutions to find the strongest problem-solution pairs. Best for early concept selection.
12. **Morphological Analysis / 形态图法**   
Breaks a product into independent dimensions and explores systematic combinations. Best for expanding design space without relying on intuition alone.
13. **Innovation Diffusion Curve / 创新扩散曲线**   
Frames adoption from innovators through laggards so product and messaging can match the current audience stage. Best for go-to-market and sequencing adoption strategy.
14. **Elevator Pitch Template / 电梯演讲模板**   
Compresses target user, problem, solution, and differentiation into a short structured story. Best for product narrative discipline.



## Group 4:Validation / 验证测试

1. **Lean Startup MVP / 精益创业MVP**   
Uses the smallest viable product or experiment to test the riskiest assumption fast. Best when conviction is low and waste would be expensive.
2. **A/B Testing / A/B 测试框架**   
Tests alternative variants with controlled experiments and statistical comparison. Best for tuning product decisions where behavior can be measured at scale.
3. **Hypothesis Board / 假设验证板**   
Turns beliefs into explicit hypotheses, tests, and outcomes. Best for making product bets legible and reviewable.
4. **5-User Testing Rule / 用户测试五法则**   
Leans on the idea that a small number of usability tests can reveal a large share of major issues early. Best for fast UX feedback before heavy build-out.
5. **Wizard of Oz Test / Wizard of Oz 测试**   
Makes the experience look automated while humans operate it behind the scenes. Best for validating demand before building automation.
6. **North Star Metric / 北极星指标框架**   
Chooses one metric that best represents recurring value delivered to users. Best for aligning experimentation and prioritization around long-term product value.
7. **Validation Learning Loop / 验证学习循环**   
Runs build → measure → learn loops and explicitly decides whether to persevere or pivot. Best for disciplined discovery rather than ad hoc experimentation.
8. **Lean Canvas / 精益画布**   
Summarizes problem, solution, unfair advantage, channels, metrics, and economics on one page. Best for startup-style business-model validation.
9. **Problem-Solution Fit / 问题-方案契合**   
Checks whether the problem is real, painful, and important—and whether the proposed solution actually addresses it. Best before chasing product-market fit.
10. **Task Completion Rate Test / 任务完成率测试**   
Measures whether users can successfully finish a core task unaided. Best for objective usability benchmarking.
11. **Idea Feasibility Threshold / 创意可行性门槛**   
Defines the minimum bar an idea must clear before it earns more resources. Best for filtering inflated ideation pipelines.
12. **HEART Framework / HEART 框架**   
Google’s UX measurement model covering Happiness, Engagement, Adoption, Retention, and Task success. Best for turning UX quality into trackable metrics.
13. **PURE Usability Evaluation / PURE 可用性评估**   
Expert review lens for utility, usability, reliability, and experience. Best for fast heuristic quality checks when user testing is not yet practical.
14. **Lean UX Canvas / Lean UX 画布**   
Combines assumptions, outcomes, MVP thinking, and learning goals on one artifact. Best for aligning cross-functional discovery work.



## Group 5:Execution / 执行落地

1. **User Story Map / 用户故事地图**   
Organizes work by user activities horizontally and delivery depth vertically. Best for seeing scope, sequencing releases, and keeping teams user-centered.
2. **RICE / RICE 优先级模型**   
Scores work by reach, impact, confidence, and effort. Best for defensible prioritization under resource pressure.
3. **MoSCoW / MoSCoW 方法**   
Sorts scope into Must, Should, Could, and Won’t. Best for fast alignment on release boundaries.
4. **Impact–Effort Matrix / Impact Effort 矩阵**   
Visualizes quick wins versus expensive low-return work. Best for rough-cut prioritization.
5. **Sprint Framework / Sprint 冲刺框架**   
Time-boxes work into short cycles with explicit goals and review points. Best for operational rhythm even outside formal Scrum.
6. **Google Design Sprint / Google Design Sprint**   
A compressed multi-day flow for framing, sketching, prototyping, and testing ideas. Best when a big bet needs an answer fast.
7. **Shape Up / Shape Up**   
Basecamp’s fixed-time, variable-scope delivery model built around shaped bets. Best for product teams that want fewer endless backlogs and more bounded bets.
8. **Now–Next–Later / NOW-NEXT-LATER**   
A roadmap format that emphasizes sequencing instead of false precision by date. Best for communicating direction amid uncertainty.
9. **DACI / DACI 决策框架**   
Clarifies driver, approver, contributors, and informed stakeholders. Best for reducing decision ambiguity and meeting thrash.
10. **ICE Scoring / ICE 评分模型**   
Prioritizes by impact, confidence, and ease. Best for quick triage when a lighter-weight score than RICE is enough.
11. **OGSM / OGSM 战略规划**   
Links objective, goals, strategies, and measures into one cascade. Best for strategy-to-execution alignment.
12. **SNAP Decision Framework / SNAP 决策框架**   
A lightweight decision screen emphasizing simplicity, value, alignment, and priority. Best for sales/product choices that need a fast, practical check.
13. **Feature Priority Pyramid / 特性优先级金字塔**   
Layers foundational, performance, and differentiating capabilities so roadmaps don’t overinvest in shiny top-layer features too soon. Best for staged roadmap design.
14. **Requirements Priority Quadrants / 需求优先级四象限**   
Sorts items by importance and urgency to distinguish immediate, planned, delegated, and deprioritized work. Best for taming noisy stakeholder demand.
15. **User Feedback Loop / 用户反馈闭环**   
Connects capture → categorization → prioritization → action → user communication. Best for making feedback operations actually improve retention and trust.



## Group 6:Growth / 增长策略

1. **AARRR Pirates / AARRR 海盗指标**   
Breaks growth into acquisition, activation, retention, referral, and revenue. Best for finding where the funnel leaks.
2. **Growth Flywheel / 增长飞轮**   
Looks for self-reinforcing loops where one gain drives the next. Best for compounding growth beyond paid acquisition.
3. **Hook Model / Hook 上钩模型**   
Designs trigger → action → variable reward → investment loops to encourage habit formation. Best for repeat-use products where habits matter.
4. **PMF / PMF 框架**   
Assesses whether the product solves a meaningful problem well enough for a specific market to pull for it. Best for deciding whether to optimize growth or keep iterating the core offer.
5. **Competitive Positioning Map / 竞争定位地图**   
Plots your offer against competitors on the dimensions users care about most. Best for finding whitespace and sharpening messaging.
6. **Blue Ocean Strategy / 蓝海策略（ERRC）**   
Escapes head-to-head competition by eliminating, reducing, raising, and creating value factors. Best for category redesign and strategic repositioning.
7. **LTV/CAC / LTV-CAC 框架**   
Compares lifetime value against acquisition cost to judge the economics of growth. Best for channel allocation and board-level growth discipline.
8. **Go-to-Market Strategy / GTM 上市策略**   
Defines target audience, message, channel, launch motion, and sales path from build to market traction. Best for new offers and major releases.
9. **Network Effects / 网络效应**   
Each added user increases value for others, strengthening defensibility if the loop is real. Best for marketplace, platform, and social products.
10. **StoryBrand / StoryBrand 故事品牌**   
Uses a story structure where the customer is the hero and the brand is the guide. Best for simplifying product messaging and conversion copy.
11. **STP / STP 市场定位**   
Segments a market, selects target segments, and positions an offer. Best for clarifying who to pursue and how to differentiate.
12. **Fogg Behavior Model / Fogg 行为模型**   
Behavior happens when motivation, ability, and a prompt coincide. Best for designing activation and habit-forming flows.
13. **JTBD Growth Matrix / JTBD 增长矩阵**   
Maps jobs by importance and current satisfaction to find under-served demand pockets. Best for pinpointing where growth opportunities are most compelling.
14. **Four Actions Framework / 四行动框架**   
Uses eliminate, reduce, raise, and create to redesign a value curve. Best as the operating tool inside Blue Ocean work.
15. **Ansoff Matrix / Ansoff 增长矩阵**   
Frames growth through market penetration, market development, product development, and diversification. Best for strategic growth-option comparison.



## Systems thinking / 系统思维

1. **First Principles / 第一性原理**   
Reduces a problem to irreducible truths and rebuilds from there. Best when industry convention has calcified bad assumptions.
2. **Wardley Mapping / Wardley 战略地图**   
Maps user needs to value-chain components and their stage of evolution. Best for technology strategy, make-vs-buy, and anticipating commoditization.
3. **Three Horizons / Three Horizons**   
Balances today’s core business, medium-term growth engines, and long-term bets. Best for portfolio and innovation planning.
4. **Platform Ecosystem Canvas / 平台生态系统画布**   
Defines participants, exchanges, incentives, rules, and health of a multi-sided platform. Best for marketplace or ecosystem design.
5. **Business Model Canvas / 商业模式画布**   
Summarizes customer, value, channel, revenue, cost, and key-resource logic on one page. Best for business-model clarity and comparison.
6. **Product Lifecycle Management / 产品生命周期管理**   
Adjusts strategy to whether a product is in introduction, growth, maturity, or decline. Best for portfolio and investment timing decisions.
7. **Antifragile Design / 反脆弱设计**   
Builds systems that get stronger under stress instead of merely surviving it. Best for resilient product architecture and risk strategy.
8. **Theory of Constraints / 约束理论**   
Finds and elevates the bottleneck that limits system throughput. Best for operational improvement and delivery-flow optimization.
9. **System Archetypes / 系统基模**   
Uses recurring structural patterns—like fixes that fail or tragedy of the commons—to spot predictable system traps. Best for diagnosing repeating organizational dysfunction.
10. **Innovation Portfolio Balance / 创新组合平衡**   
Balances exploitation of the core with exploration of future options. Best for leadership teams managing horizon tradeoffs.
11. **Porter Five Forces / 波特五力模型**   
Assesses structural profitability through supplier power, buyer power, rivalry, substitution, and new-entry threat. Best for industry attractiveness analysis.
12. **Value Chain Analysis / 价值链分析**   
Breaks the business into primary and support activities to locate where value and differentiation are created. Best for cost, strategy, and moat analysis.

### 

### If you are in discovery

Start with:

* Design Thinking
* JTBD
* User Journey Map
* Empathy Map
* Contextual Inquiry
* Affinity Diagram

### 

### If you are sharpening the problem

Start with:

* HMW / POV / Problem Tree
* Double Diamond
* Iceberg Model
* Cynefin
* Competitor Analysis

### 

### If you are generating directions

Start with:

* SCAMPER
* Crazy 8s
* Analogical Thinking
* Opportunity Solution Tree
* Value Proposition Canvas

### 

### If you are validating

Start with:

* Lean Startup MVP
* Hypothesis Board
* A/B Testing
* Problem-Solution Fit
* HEART / Task Completion Rate / PURE

### 

### If you are prioritizing and shipping

Start with:

* User Story Map
* RICE or ICE
* MoSCoW
* Impact–Effort
* Shape Up / Sprint / Now–Next–Later / DACI

### 

### If you are driving growth

Start with:

* AARRR
* PMF
* LTV/CAC
* STP
* GTM
* Network Effects
* Growth Flywheel

### 

### If you are setting strategy

Start with:

* First Principles
* Wardley Mapping

* Business Model Canvas
* Theory of Constraints
* Porter Five Forces
* Value Chain Analysis
